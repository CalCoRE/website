Making Climate Justice Count
news

Michelle was honored to provide the Foreword to our colleagues Asli Sezen-Barie, Marie K. Stapleton, and Hosun Kang’s just released book, Making Climate Justice Count: Teaching with Data Stories for Action. The book features case studies, practical strategies, and a strong overarching framework for considering how students can engage deeply with climate data throughout the science curriculum.

CoRE Lab to Present Rivulet Framework at ISLS 2026
news rivulet

The CoRE Lab is excited to present our work on the Rivulet design framework at the upcoming 2026 Annual Meeting of the International Society for the Learning Sciences (ISLS). Rivulet is a python-based data pipeline designed to help educators, curriculum designers, and researchers extract pedagogically generative datasets from online telemetry portals (such as EPA air quality and USGS water quality databases).

Layered Landscapes in Palgrave Handbook of Climate Change Education
news otaca wds

Jake, Helen, and Michelle contributed a chapter called Layered Landscapes: Critical Climate Cartography as a Pedagogical Framework to Bring Together Data, Experience, Science, and History to the recently published Palgrave Handbook of Climate Change Research in Transdisciplinary Education, edited by Asli Sezen-Barie and Sara Tolbert.

2025

New Publication: Being 'Choosy' in Teaching Statistics with Big Data
news choosy

We are pleased to announce our latest publication, which explores strategies for making large datasets manageable and pedagogically generative for K-12 educators. Published in December 2025, Being ‘Choosy’ in Teaching Statistics with Big Data provides a systematic approach for curriculum developers to downsample and structure public datasets so that they align with GAISE II data literacy standards.

CoRE Lab at ISLS 2025
news

The CoRE Lab presented several research papers and interactive symposia at the 2025 Annual Meeting of the International Society for the Learning Sciences (ISLS). Our presentations highlighted new methodologies for integrating statistical coding in teacher training and exploring computational modeling in classrooms. Thank you to everyone who attended and discussed our sessions!
