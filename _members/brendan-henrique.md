I am a 3rd year Ph.D. Student in the Learning Sciences Cluster at the Berkeley School of Education. I have a M.A. in Urban Education from Loyola Marymount University and I was previously a 7th grade science teacher in Richmond, CA.

My research explores critically conscious STEM education with special attention to computer science education. My latest project involved the design and implementation of a critically conscious pre-service teacher computer science course.

I am a part of the Sustained Modeling in K12 Science (A2S) project where I work on the curriculum design team as well as the research team.
