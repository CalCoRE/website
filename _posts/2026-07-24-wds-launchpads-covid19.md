This launchpad provides students with structured epidemiological data tracking cases, vaccination rates, and mortality statistics from the COVID-19 pandemic. It introduces key concepts in public health data representation and disease transmission modeling.

**Intended Audience & Use Case:** 
Designed for middle and high school statistics, health science, and biology teachers. It helps students understand exponential growth, interpret health metrics, and construct comparisons of local and national infection trends inside CODAP.
