This launchpad utilizes California's CalEnviroScreen dataset to explore cumulative environmental pollution burdens. It allows students to map and analyze various health, environmental, and demographic metrics across different California census tracts.

**Intended Audience & Use Case:** 
Designed for middle and high school science, social studies, and environmental justice educators. It provides a structured workflow for students to query socioeconomic variables, particulate pollution, and local health metrics to construct data-driven arguments about environmental inequality in CODAP.
