The CoRE Lab is excited to present our work on the **Rivulet** design framework at the upcoming **2026 Annual Meeting of the International Society for the Learning Sciences (ISLS)**. Rivulet is a python-based data pipeline designed to help educators, curriculum designers, and researchers extract pedagogically generative datasets from online telemetry portals (such as EPA air quality and USGS water quality databases).

In our presentation, we will discuss our design methodologies and share findings on how local, recent, and statistically structured environmental telemetry supports authentic, place-based data inquiry in secondary science classrooms.
