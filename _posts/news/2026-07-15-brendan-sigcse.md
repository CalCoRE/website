Brendan's project “Teaching the CS Teachers: Examining the Design, Motivation, and Curricular Changes in CS Teacher Preparation Programs” was selected to receive a Special Projects grant from the ACM Special Interest Group in Computer Science Education in the amount of $5000.

This award will support Brendan in investigating how Computer Science teacher educators are adjusting their curriculum and instruction in response to the rapidly changing sociotechnical landscape of computing. He is particularly interested in what motivates teacher educators to change their materials, and how this can inform future policy and practice.
