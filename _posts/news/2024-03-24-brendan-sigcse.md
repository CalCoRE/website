Congratulations to Brendan Henrique, who won **first place** in the Graduate Student Research competition at the **ACM Technical Symposium on Computer Science Education (SIGCSE 2024)**!

Brendan's research explored computational notebooks (such as Jupyter) in K-12 teacher professional development, evaluating how first-hand coding and data investigation experiences help teachers make informed decisions about incorporating computing in their classrooms.
